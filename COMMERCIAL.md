# Commercial use and licensing

Short version: free for students, teachers, and anyone not making money from it.
If you are making money from it, you need a license. Email `license@mypinaka.com`.

## The two licenses, and what they actually allow

pinaka-abhyas ships two things under two licenses.

**The application code** is under GNU AGPL-3.0 (`LICENSE`). AGPL does not forbid
commercial use. You may run a paid service on this code, provided you meet every AGPL
obligation, including publishing the complete corresponding source of your modified
version to every user who interacts with it over a network. Most companies will not
do that. Those companies buy a commercial license instead.

**The question content** is under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (`LICENSE-CONTENT.md`). The
NonCommercial term means commercial use is not licensed at all. No amount of
compliance unlocks it. You need a commercial license from the Licensor.

The questions are the part most people want. Assume you need a license unless one of
the free cases below clearly covers you.

## What counts as commercial use

Creative Commons deliberately leaves "NonCommercial" broad, and permits the licensor
to state their own interpretation. This section is that statement. It governs how the
Licensor reads the NC term.

**Commercial use** means use primarily intended for or directed toward commercial
advantage or monetary compensation, whether or not money changes hands for the
content itself.

### Free. No license needed.

- A student practising, alone or with friends.
- A teacher or tutor using items in classes offered at no charge.
- A school, college, or university using the app with its own students at no
  additional charge to them.
- A library, NGO, or government programme offering free preparation.
- A researcher studying the items, the engine, or the learning data.
- A developer forking the app for a personal or non-commercial project.
- Quoting items with attribution in a free blog post, video, or forum answer, even
  if that channel carries incidental platform advertising. Incidental ad revenue on
  otherwise free educational material is not the kind of monetisation this term is
  aimed at.
- Translating items into another language and publishing the translation free, under
  the same CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 terms.

### Needs a commercial license.

- A coaching institute, tuition centre, or test-prep company using items in any
  material connected to a paid course, including free demo classes that market a paid
  course. Charging students a fee makes the use commercial even if the items are
  handed out at no separate charge.
- Any app, website, or product that ships the content and charges money, runs a
  subscription, gates features behind payment, or is advertising-supported as its
  business model.
- Printing items in a book, workbook, or question bank offered for sale.
- Using items in paid corporate or professional training.
- Reselling, sublicensing, or redistributing the content as a paid dataset.
- Using the content as training data for a machine-learning model that is sold,
  licensed, or offered as a paid service.
- Running a service on the application code without meeting AGPL-3.0 source
  disclosure obligations.

### Not sure

Email `license@mypinaka.com` and describe the use. A direct answer costs you one
email and removes the risk. Asking is free and always will be.

## What a commercial license gives you

A commercial license is negotiated per use. Depending on what you need, it can grant:

- The right to use the question content commercially, without the NonCommercial
  restriction.
- An exception to AGPL-3.0 copyleft for the application code, so you may run or
  distribute a modified version without publishing your source.
- Relief from the ShareAlike obligation on content derivatives you create.
- A defined support and update arrangement.

It does not grant rights to the Pinaka trademarks. Those are covered separately in
`TRADEMARK.md`, and are not licensed by default under any agreement here.

Pricing depends on scale and use. Email `license@mypinaka.com` with what you want to
do and roughly how many students it reaches.

## Why the line is drawn here

Every question in this bank was authored from the public syllabus and machine-verified
before it shipped. That work is funded by nothing. Keeping it free for students, and
charging the businesses that would otherwise sell it back to those same students, is
what pays for the next thousand questions.

If you are a student and money is the reason you would not use this, it is free. It
will stay free.

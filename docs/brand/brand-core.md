# Brand core

The exam-agnostic doctrine. Copied from the paid LSAT flagship brand, because it is mature and correct. This is the part that does not change between exams. Exam-specific positioning lives in `positioning-ca.md`.

This mirrors the schema. Brand core is to positioning what the UQS Core is to a Profile: one stable spine, many specializations.

## Name

Pinaka is Sanskrit. It is Shiva's bow, the weapon used for the destruction of ignorance. The chevron mark is the bow.

## Brand idea

Taught by testing. Not content delivery. Not coaching. Diagnostic-led testing as the way of learning. The test does the teaching.

## Voice: The Fellow

A peer with experience. Knowledgeable but not lecturing. Direct but not cold. Enough rigour to diagnose without hedging. Enough warmth to land without wounding. Never patronising. Plain language is a courtesy, not a default; it speaks to someone capable of reading the diagnosis.

## The seven principles

1. Precision over persuasion. Numbers beat adjectives.
2. Reveal, do not explain. The test does the talking.
3. Earned moments. Nothing given before the work is done.
4. One answer per screen. Two questions, split the surface.
5. The artifact is the ad. Build it to be screenshotted.
6. Subtract until it breaks, then add back one. Default to removing.
7. Respect the reader. No hype, no rhetorical questions, no em-dashes.

## Non-negotiables

- No em-dashes. Not the long dash, not the double hyphen.
- No exclamation marks.
- No rhetorical questions as transitions. "Ready to see your results" is banned.
- State, do not sell.
- No dark patterns. No lying. Add value before any extraction.

## Sentence shape

- Short.
- Pair a strength with a cost. "You move fast. Your accuracy on hard problems drops when you do."
- Use numbers when you have them. "You lost four marks to careless sign errors, not to the hard topics."
- Write "do not", not "don't".
- Never open with "Imagine", "Picture this", or a rhetorical question.
- Never end on an exclamation mark.

## Visual direction

Sparse outside, dense inside. The marketing surface is calm and almost empty. The product surface is information-dense, because the diagnosis is the value. Cool neutral base, one brand signal colour, one accent. Type-led. No stock photography, no decorative illustration.

## When in doubt

State, do not sell. Subtract until it breaks, then add back one. The artifact is the ad. The reader is intelligent and busy.

# Positioning: CA Foundation (Pinaka Abhyas)

The exam-specific layer on top of `brand-core.md`. The voice, the seven principles, and the visual system in `design.md` are inherited unchanged. What changes is who we speak to, what we call things, what we lead with, and the reading level. The LSAT positioning does not transfer. Only the doctrine does.

## The product, in one line

A free, open-source, offline-capable app where a CA Foundation aspirant practises verified questions and learns what they do not know, per topic and per misconception.

## Who it is for

A self-studying CA Foundation aspirant in India. Often a commerce student, late teens to early twenties, preparing alongside coaching or alone. Time-pressed. Anxious about a large syllabus. Wants to know, honestly, where they stand and what to do next. Treat them as capable. They are sitting one of the harder entry exams in the country. Many of them read English as a second language.

## What we lead with

Free for students. Open-source app. Works offline. Your data stays on your device, and sharing is opt-in and anonymous. This is not a feature list bolted on. It is the position. We are not selling anything. We are trying to add value to students who are often sold to.

## Vocabulary

Use: topic, subtopic, misconception, problem, practice, mock, drill, skill, mastery, readiness, diagnosis, review.

Do not use: anything LSAT-specific (LR, RC, the 120 to 180 scale). Any pricing language, because the app is free. "Predicted score" is banned until we can back it with data. Say "readiness estimate" and label it honestly as an estimate.

The India-specific banned terms in `brand-core.md` apply with full force here. No guarantee language, no "topper", no "crack", no scarcity theater, no "shortcut" or "tricks", no competition-fear framing. Use "clear" not "crack", "methods" not "tricks".

## What readiness means here

The Paper 3 pass bar is 40 out of 100, net of negative marking (plus one per correct, minus a quarter per wrong). Readiness is shown as honest distance to that bar, as an estimate, never as a promise. We do not invent a predicted mark we cannot stand behind.

## CA reading level

Per the reading-level policy in `brand-core.md`, this pack sets its own ceiling.

Target CEFR B1. Ceiling CEFR B2. No idioms. Many users read English as a second language, so plain, literal phrasing is a courtesy, not a dilution. The universal sentence doctrine still holds: short sentences, numbers over adjectives, "do not" not "don't", no em-dashes, no exclamation marks. Reading level constrains word choice and clause depth. It does not relax the voice.

When a sentence needs an idiom or a rare word to work, rewrite the sentence.

## ICAI trademark posture

Conservative, mirroring the flagship's pattern for LSAC.

CA Foundation is an examination conducted by the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI). The name appears as descriptive copy only. Never "official". Never "ICAI-approved". Never "endorsed". No ICAI logo. No reproduced ICAI questions. All questions are original.

Every public surface that mentions CA Foundation carries this disclaimer:

> CA Foundation is an examination conducted by the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI). Pinaka Abhyas is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by ICAI.

The exam name never enters the wordmark or the logo lockup. See `design.md`.

## Peak architecture, instantiated for CA

Every session has one designed peak and one honest close. That pattern is doctrine, set in `brand-core.md`. Here is how it lands on CA surfaces.

The readiness reveal is the peak. Its close is honest diagnosis, then a concrete next step. No flattery. The diagnosis names the misconception and shows the numbers.

| Session | Peak | Close |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Drill | One-sentence insight from this session | Mastery node updates. Calm screen. No auto-queue. |
| Mock | Readiness estimate plus distance to the 40 bar, plus one insight | "Review queued. Start the next when ready." No auto-start. |
| Readiness screen | The estimate, as an estimate, with the distance to the pass bar | The rank-one misconception, named, with the numbers, and the next thing to drill |
| Mistake review | The misconception pattern recognized across questions | A two-sentence summary, surfaced once |

Forbidden at any close: payment prompt (there is no payment), share-this-score button, "you completed a drill" badge, XP, streak counter, leaderboard, auto-queue to the next session. The student decides the rhythm.

## Surface voice cheat-sheet

The base mix is Sage 50 / Everyman 30 / Magician 20. These surfaces tilt it. App surfaces and open-source surfaces are both brand surfaces.

| Surface | Lead voice | Peak and close notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Drill session | Sage, Everyman | One answer per screen. The insight lands at the end, plainly. No badge. |
| Mock session | Sage | Real timing, real marking. No commentary mid-session. The readout is the peak. |
| Readiness screen | Magician leads (30 / 30 / 40) | The peak. The estimate as an estimate, distance to 40, the named misconception, the next step. No flattery. |
| Mistake review | Sage plus Magician | Name the pattern across questions. Show the numbers. Do not scold. |
| Install prompt | Everyman | Plain. "Install to study offline. Your progress stays on your device." No urgency. |
| Progress export | Everyman | "Your progress is yours. Export it any time, take it anywhere." State the fact. |
| README | Sage | State what it is, what it does, what it costs (nothing). Open source as proof. No sell. |
| Release notes | Everyman plus Sage | What changed and what it costs. No hype. No exclamation marks. |
| Contributor guide | Everyman | Thank contributors plainly. Disagree with code, not people. |

## Tone check for any CA surface

State the diagnosis plainly. Name the misconception, do not scold. Show the number when there is one. No hype, no exclamation, no em-dash. Carry the ICAI disclaimer if the surface mentions CA Foundation. The student did the work. The screen reflects it back.

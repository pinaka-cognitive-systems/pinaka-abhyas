## No license by default

AGPL-3.0 grants copyright and patent rights, not trademark rights. Section 7(e) of
AGPL-3.0 expressly permits the licensor to decline trademark permission, and this
policy is that declining. CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 likewise states that no trademark rights
are granted.

A commercial license under `COMMERCIAL.md` does not grant trademark rights either
unless it says so in writing and names the marks.

So: you may fork this project freely. You may not call your fork Pinaka Abhyas.

## What you may do without asking

- **Say what your thing is built on.** "Based on Pinaka Abhyas", "a fork of Pinaka
  Abhyas", "compatible with Pinaka Abhyas question packs". Use the name as a plain
  word in a factual sentence, not as your own product name.
- **Refer to the project** in articles, reviews, tutorials, videos, academic papers,
  comparisons, and conference talks, favourable or not.
- **Link to the repository or the website.**
- **Keep the marks in place** when you redistribute the project unmodified.
- **Use the name in a course or class** where you are teaching students to use the
  software.

## What you need written permission for

- **Naming.** Using Pinaka, Abhyas, or Pinaka Abhyas, or any confusingly similar name,
  as the name of your product, service, company, app, or organisation.
- **Domains and listings.** Registering a domain name, social media handle, package
  name, or app store listing that contains the marks.
- **Logos.** Using the logo or app icons as the icon or branding of your own product,
  or modifying them for that purpose.
- **Implying endorsement.** Any use that suggests Pinaka Abhyas sponsors, endorses,
  certifies, or is affiliated with your product, service, or content. This includes
  describing a fork as "official", "certified", or "verified".
- **Merchandise.** Applying the marks to goods for sale.

## Forks must rename

If you distribute a modified version, change the name, the logo, and the app icons
before you do. This is a condition of using the marks, not of using the code: AGPL
gives you the code regardless, and this policy governs only the branding you attach
to it.

Rename the user-visible product name, the PWA manifest name, the app icons, and the
domain. Then say plainly that your version is derived from Pinaka Abhyas. That
sentence is welcome and is covered above.

## Quality matters, which is why this exists

Every shipped question is machine-verified before it ships, and the verification
record is public. The name is a claim about that standard. A fork that ships wrong
answer keys under the same name transfers its errors onto every student who trusts
the name, and there is no way to tell the two apart. That is the harm this policy
prevents. It is not about controlling who builds what.

Community exam packs distributed through this project are labelled "community pack"
and are distinct from "verified pack" for the same reason. See ADR 0007.

## Why this exists

pinaka-abhyas is free for students and open to the public under AGPL-3.0 (code) and
CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (question content). Businesses that want terms those licenses do not
grant buy a separate commercial license from the Licensor. See `COMMERCIAL.md`.

Selling that commercial license requires the Licensor to hold sufficient rights over
the whole work. Without this agreement, a contributor keeps rights the Licensor cannot
grant onward, and the commercial license becomes unsellable over the affected parts.
This agreement prevents that.

It does not take your copyright. You keep it. You are giving the Licensor a broad
license alongside your own ownership.

## How to sign

Open your pull request. A bot will comment asking you to sign. Reply with a comment
containing exactly this line:

```
I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA
```

Your signature is recorded against your GitHub account and covers all your future
contributions to this repository. You will not be asked again.

Pull requests cannot merge until the CLA check is green.
